# Data Privacy

SupportAI stores account emails through Supabase Auth, business profile data, uploaded support documents, extracted chunks, conversations, and analytics events.

Data is used to provide customer support answers and dashboard analytics. The MVP is scoped for US + Pakistan launch planning. Public launch requires legal review of retention periods, deletion workflows, privacy policy, terms of service, and third-party data processing terms.

Do not upload health, payment-card, government ID, or other regulated sensitive data without a specific compliance review.
