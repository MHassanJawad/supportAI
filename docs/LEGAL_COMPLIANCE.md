# Legal Compliance

This MVP includes placeholder documentation only and is not legal advice.

- GDPR: Not first-launch scope, but required before targeting EU users.
- CCPA: Review before targeting California consumers.
- PCI-DSS: Not applicable because payments are out of scope.
- HIPAA: Not applicable unless health data is processed; do not support health data without BAAs.
- SOC 2: Future B2B trust requirement.

Terms, privacy policy, and cookie policy require lawyer review before public launch.
